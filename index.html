Alzheimer's Detection Using Deep Learning

An academic research project that uses EfficientNetV2M to classify Alzheimer's severity from MRI scans and compares it with four other architectures.

This is an academic research project. It is not a clinically validated diagnostic system and does not replace medical professionals.

PythonTensorFlow/KerasEfficientNetV2MSPSS
MRI scans→Data augmentation→Transfer learning→Model comparison→SPSS analysis

Results stated on my resume

  • EfficientNetV2M outperformed DenseNet201, ResNet152, NasNetLarge and ConvNeXtLarge on accuracy, precision, recall and F1-score
  • Statistical analysis in SPSS supported research documentation and publication

Research objective

Classify the severity of Alzheimer's disease from MRI scans using deep learning.
